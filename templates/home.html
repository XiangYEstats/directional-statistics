01 / UNDERSTAND THE GEOMETRY

A circle has
no natural beginning.

Compass bearings and times of day wrap around. Averaging their numerical labels can be misleading: the arithmetic mean of 359° and 1° is 180°, while their circular mean is 0°.

A rose diagram makes the pattern visible by grouping observations into angular sectors. In the diagram above, larger sector areas mean more observations.
